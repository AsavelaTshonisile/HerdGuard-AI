# HerdGuard AI - South African Regulatory Alignment & Medical Guardrails

## 1. Statutory Context & Legislation
HerdGuard AI is designed to operate within the South African agricultural and animal health regulatory environment, primarily governed by:
- **Animal Diseases Act (Act No. 35 of 1984)** and relevant subordinate regulations.
- **Veterinary and Para-Veterinary Professions Act (Act No. 19 of 1982)**.
- **Fertilizers, Farm Feeds, Agricultural Remedies and Stock Remedies Act (Act No. 36 of 1947)**.

---

## 2. Complementary Positioning (Non-Replacement Principle)
HerdGuard AI is positioned strictly as a **complementary, early-awareness, and farm-level health management platform**:
1. **Government Systems**: The platform does NOT claim to replace statutory disease reporting channels, official quarantine directives, or the National Department of Agriculture, Land Reform and Rural Development (DALRRD).
2. **Veterinary Practitioners**: The system does NOT replace the diagnostic examination, auscultation, clinical judgment, or prescriptions of registered veterinarians or authorized Animal Health Technicians (AHTs).
3. **Traceability Infrastructure**: The system supports individual farm management and works alongside national livestock identification initiatives.

---

## 3. Medical and Diagnostic Guardrails
1. **Preliminary Assessments Only**: All outputs produced by the rules engine or future machine-learning models are designated as **preliminary syndromic risk assessments**.
2. **No Prescription of Medicines**: The application does not recommend specific pharmaceutical dosages, prescription antimicrobial regimens, or controlled stock remedies.
3. **Veterinary Referral Advisory**: For assessments categorized as `High Risk` or `Urgent Attention Required`, the system immediately displays instructions to isolate the affected animals and contact the local State Veterinarian.
4. **No Artificial Diagnostic Precision**: The platform deliberately avoids publishing misleading or fabricated diagnostic accuracy percentages (e.g. "98.7% confident this is Anthrax").

---

## 4. Verification Hierarchy for Disease Intelligence
To protect farmers from panic and prevent misinformation, HerdGuard AI enforces a three-tier classification for geographical disease incidents:
1. **Suspected**: Farmer-reported clinical observations that have not yet undergone professional on-site veterinary evaluation.
2. **Professionally Reviewed**: Clinical observations investigated and logged by an authorized Animal Health Technician or registered veterinarian with diagnostic samples collected.
3. **Official Outbreak**: Confirmed statutory disease outbreaks formally gazetted or notified by the provincial or national veterinary authority.
